# Evidence search record

This project uses a documented narrative search, not a systematic or scoping review. The first comprehensive search and source-status check was completed on 18–19 July 2026. A full refresh followed on 20 November 2026.

## Coverage

Searches covered per-request energy, carbon and water; task and model variation; data-centre electricity and water; model development; hardware materials and e-waste; lifecycle assessment methods; EU reporting rules; Czech infrastructure; and wider digital-use context.

The search prioritised official statistics and reports, peer-reviewed work, authoritative technical standards and primary legal sources. Provider disclosures and preprints were retained only with explicit labels. Unsourced aggregations, viral equivalence claims and values with unrecoverable boundaries were excluded.

## Stop rule

The search stopped when every displayed material claim had a direct supporting source and locator, current sector reports had replaced superseded figures, source status had been checked, and additional sources would broaden the topic without changing the conclusion.

## November 2026 refresh

Nine parallel searches covered: per-request energy, carbon and water; global sector totals; national and local concentration; water; hardware, e-waste and model development; EU rules; the Czech context; assessment methods and wider digital use; and the status of every registered source. Each search prioritised material published or changed after 1 July 2026 and reported what it could not reach.

Outcome: two displayed statements overtaken, one product renamed, two statements added (coverage of the first EU reporting period; no Czech data in it), no correction or retraction of any registered source. Candidate sources that would extend the page without correcting it are held in `WATCH_LIST.md`.

Excluded in this refresh: a consultancy forecast of Czech data-centre consumption available only as a press statement without a published method; a per-prompt water figure traceable only to press coverage; a placeholder regulation number printed in Commission footnotes.

## Refresh rule

A full refresh is triggered by new knowledge, not by the calendar. The triggers and the monthly watch are defined in `docs/UPDATE_PROTOCOL.md`; open items are kept in `WATCH_LIST.md`. Record new decisions in the source registry and claim-evidence matrix.
