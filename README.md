# AI Environmental Footprint

A bilingual, evidence-based explainer about the energy, water, materials and infrastructure behind AI use.

[Read in English](https://michalie.github.io/ai-environmental-footprint/) · [Číst česky](https://michalie.github.io/ai-environmental-footprint/cs.html)

## About

The environmental impact of AI cannot be reduced to a single number per prompt. It depends on the task, model, infrastructure, location, date and what the estimate includes.

The website presents current evidence in context, distinguishes measurements from models and forecasts, and avoids comparisons between values that describe different systems.

## Evidence

Material claims are linked to their sources, limitations and relevant system boundaries. Calculations and evidence decisions are documented in the repository.

Source full texts are retained locally by the maintainers and are not redistributed here.

## Languages

The English and Czech versions use the same evidence, values and citations. The Czech version is written as natural academic Czech rather than as a literal translation.

## Maintenance

Instructions for updating or adapting the site are available in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and the [update protocol](docs/UPDATE_PROTOCOL.md). Anyone changing factual content must obtain and check the relevant full texts.

## Scope

This is an educational explainer, not a systematic review or an independent lifecycle assessment. All claims were last read against their full texts on **19 July 2026**. A full refresh on **20 November 2026** rechecked the status of every source and read every new or changed claim against its full text.

The project was developed with AI assistance and human scientific and editorial oversight. It has not undergone independent external scientific review.
