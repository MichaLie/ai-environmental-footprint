# Evidence policy

The explainer prioritises official measurements and statistics, peer-reviewed research, authoritative technical reports and binding legal texts. Preprints and provider disclosures may be used when their status and limits are explicit and they answer a question not better covered by stronger evidence.

Search snippets, abstracts and secondary retellings can help locate a source but cannot support a material claim. A DOI or a valid file hash confirms identity or integrity, not claim support.

Every included claim must retain its population or system, method, date, functional unit, boundary and uncertainty. Measurements, models, scenarios and forecasts remain visibly distinct. Values from different systems are not converted into equivalence ratios simply because their units can be made to match.

Full texts are retained locally by the maintainers and are not redistributed in this repository. The public registry records the verified file size and hash so a local evidence snapshot can be checked without exposing its path.

`PASS` means that the maintainers checked the claim against the cited source and locator. It does not mean independent replication, external peer review of this explainer or proof that a source will remain unchanged.
