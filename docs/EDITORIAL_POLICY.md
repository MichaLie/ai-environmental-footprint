# Editorial policy

The site is written for academic and informed public readers. It should be precise without sounding like a technical audit report.

- Lead with what the evidence allows readers to conclude.
- Keep qualifications beside the claim they limit.
- Name whether a value is measured, self-reported, modelled or projected.
- Avoid rhetorical certainty, false balance and dramatic equivalence claims.
- Use plain language where it remains accurate; explain necessary specialist terms on first use.
- Keep the English version natural and the Czech version idiomatic rather than translating sentence by sentence.
- Do not expose build notes, validation counts or maintenance history in the public pages.

The two versions may differ in sentence structure, but not in evidence strength, numbers, source identity or material caveats.
